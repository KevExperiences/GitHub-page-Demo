Karnaugh Map Rules

Goal: Cover all 1s using the fewest and largest rectangular boxes possible.
  • Power of 2: Group sizes must be 1, 2, 4, 8, or 16 cells.
  • Rectangles Only: Only filled 1x2, 2x2, 1x4, 4x4 boxes (no diagonals or L-shapes).
  • SOP vs POS: Switch to SOP to group 1s (Sum of Products), or POS to group 0s (Product of Sums).
  • Torus Grid (Wrap-Around): Top wraps to bottom; left wraps to right (including 4 corners).
  • Multiple Choice: Select valid cells, then click the correct product/sum term option!
  • Sandbox Mode: Right-click cells to edit value (0 → 1 → X).
